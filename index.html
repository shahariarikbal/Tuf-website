About Islamic Center

We established our center in 1954, and since then have served local families through prayer, study circles, community care, and trusted education.

Visit our premises to join regular programs, learn the Quran, and take part in initiatives built around faith and service.

Admission Process

A clear step-by-step process for parents who want to enroll their children in Tanzimul Ummah Foundation.

01

Collect Form

Choose the suitable program and collect the admission form from the office or online desk.

02

Assessment

Students complete a basic interview or placement assessment based on age and program level.

03

Documents

Submit required documents, guardian information, photos, and previous academic records.

04

Confirmation

Complete fees, receive class instructions, and join the orientation for the new session.
