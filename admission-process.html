How To Apply

Step-by-step admission guide

01

Choose Program & Campus

Select the suitable academic program, campus, class level, and residential or non-residential option.

02

Collect Or Submit Form

Collect the form from the admission office or submit the online application with guardian contact information.

03

Placement Assessment

Students may complete a short Quran reading, academic level, or interview assessment based on the program.

04

Document Verification

Submit recent photos, birth certificate or student ID, previous records, guardian NID, and emergency contact details.

05

Fee Payment & Confirmation

Complete admission fees, receive class instructions, and confirm routine, books, uniform, and orientation details.

Required Documents

  • Completed admission form
  • Two recent passport-size photos
  • Birth certificate or student ID information
  • Previous academic record or transfer certificate
  • Guardian NID and active phone number
